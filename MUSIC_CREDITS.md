lines and drum patterns are written for these tracks.

(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Not included

Soundtracks from commercial arcade or console games are copyrighted and are not bundled.
Users can play music they own by putting the files in the My Music folder
(`<app data>/music/`); those files stay on their computer and are never copied into the
app or the repository.
